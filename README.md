# Illinois Data Center Air Tracker

A public tracker of AI data centers and their associated air quality in Illinois.

## Background

Large AI data centers are being approved in Illinois with very little public information on the air pollution produced by these facilities, particularly diesel backup generators. This project aims to provide the public with an accessible source of publicly available information.

## Progress
- Building a dataset of proposed and approved data centers within Illinois.
- Mapping each data center with local population data.

## Data Sources (planned)
- City Council records and public documents.
- Illinois EPA Air Permits.
- U.S. Census.

## Current Status
Ongoing (Started November 2026).
